## Respuesta (en «Reply to App Review»; los puntos 2 a 6 también en Notes)

Thank you for your review. Here is the requested information.

1. Screen recording: attached (iPhone, latest iOS). It starts by launching the app, signs in with the
demo account, and shows the main features: profile, calendar, time-off requests, the attendance
check-in (camera and location), the AI assistant, and sign out. The app has no account registration:
accounts are created by the company for its employees, so there is no in-app sign-up flow. It has no
user-generated content shared between users and no paid content.

2. Purpose and audience: SistemasSI is the internal app of SI SOL Inmobiliarias, S.A. de C.V., a real
estate company in Mexico. It is used only by our employees (about 80 active users in Mexico and Spain)
to check in and out of work with photo and location, request vacation and time off, see their profile
and assigned equipment, read company announcements, use the shared calendar and appointments, and ask
questions to the company AI assistant. It replaces paper forms and spreadsheets and lets employees do
these tasks from their phone.

3. Access: sign in with the demo account provided in App Review Information (system.sisol@gmail.com).
It is a regular employee account without administrator permissions. Camera and location are only
requested in the Attendance (Asistencia) section; they can be denied and the rest of the app keeps
working. No sample files are needed.

4. External services: Supabase (database, file storage and authentication), Cloudflare (web hosting of
the same system at sistemassi.com), Ollama Cloud (AI model that answers questions to the assistant),
Microsoft Power BI (embedded reports for some areas), Google Drive (work documents), and WhatsApp
(employees can also reach the assistant from an authorized number). There are no payment processors
and no in-app purchases.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in Mexico and Spain, the only countries where
it is available.

6. Regulated industry: not applicable. The app does not provide financial, medical or other regulated
services, and it does not include protected third-party content.

Because the app is intended only for our employees, we are also requesting Unlisted App Distribution
for it.
